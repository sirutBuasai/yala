Question 5

What the history summary shows

The summary never looks at which filters you set. It looks at what is left in the list, so any combination of filters is covered by the same few rules.

When the filtered list holdsThe summary shows
AnythingHow many rows
TransactionsSpent: their sum, refunds netted
PaychecksTake-home: their sum
Bill pay & transfersBill pay & transfers: their sum
Transactions from exactly one categoryAlso vs your average for that category, the same figure as its row in the categories chart
Rows touching exactly one cardAlso Charged − paid: what went on the card less what was paid to it

So: filtering to Takeouts on Amex Gold shows Rows, Spent and vs your average (one category) but not Charged − paid, because Amex Gold's payments are bill pay, which the Takeouts filter removed. Search, Pending only and several categories at once never add a figure; they only narrow what the figures add up.
