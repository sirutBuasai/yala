What to look at

  • Controls beside chartsDecision 1. On the page, the assumptions are the page's main content, so they sit in a pane next to what they move rather than behind Adjust. The dashed outlines on the Was tab mark what moves to Planning.
  • Save and DiscardIn Financial planning's header, as they were in the modal's. Save changes is enabled only while a control differs from the ledger. In the modal, closing after a save was the confirmation; in a pane, Save just goes grey again. Say if you want a confirmation line there, which would be a new string.
  • What previewsEvery pane on the page redraws from the draft: the projection, the depletion line, Financial progress, and Years of freedom's target. Years of freedom's own figure stays put when you change Planned spending, since it reads logged spending (decision 4).
  • Projection frameFixed at the largest FI number the withdrawal slider allows, so dragging the withdrawal rate moves the FI line inside the plot instead of rescaling it. Lines above that level are clipped, as today.
  • LayoutA proposed default only. You arrange it in Edit, and your arrangement becomes the default (D51).
  • New UI stringsNone.
