Yala Redesign
Yala · layout proposal · September 2026

One focus month, every page in step

A Monarch-style reorganization of Yala around the two jobs you actually do: logging and checking a month, and reading trends across months. The period you look at becomes a selection you click, not a tab you switch.

What goes wrong today

The views are split by time range, the work is not

Home, Activity and Net Worth each hold their own Month, Year and All time boards with their own remembered period. Logging a transaction on Home and then checking it means visiting Activity · Month for the list and Activity · Year for the category heatmap, then stepping the month by hand when an outlier shows up.

The spreadsheets never had that problem, because a month sheet and the year summary were one tab apart and the same numbers. The mapping below keeps each spreadsheet habit and gives it one home.

Principles

Four rules the layout follows

Select a period, don't switch to it

One focus month is shared by every page and kept in the URL. Charts always show the whole year around it; clicking a bar, a cell or a chart point moves it.

Every figure opens its transactions

A category cell, a bar or a flagged outlier opens the transaction list filtered to exactly the rows behind that number. A back link returns you to where you were.

Check your work where you log it

The transaction page carries the month's totals, each category against its typical month, and the year strip. A wrong amount or category shows up the moment you save.

Net worth explains itself

Each month's net worth change splits into what you saved, from your transactions, and what markets and everything else did. That is the bridge between the two files.

Scope

What goes away and what stays

  • Month, Year and All time tabs on Activity and Net Worth.
  • Four separately remembered periods (Home month, Activity month, Activity year, Net Worth year), replaced by one focus month in the URL.
  • Separate Transactions, Paychecks, Bill pay & transfers and Pending panes, replaced by one list with type filters.
  • The Home tab as a logging hub; logging moves to Transactions, and quick add works from every page.
  • Every existing chart, the catalog and the data.json contract. The redesign regroups figures; it does not need new API fields except, later, a per-account balance series for sparklines.
  • The entry modals and guided forms, reused by quick add and the inline add row.
  • Balance reconciliation for cards, surfaced as a check on Log balances and in Needs attention.
  • Planning and Manage, moved into the sidebar as Planning and Settings.
Build order

Five steps, each shippable alone

  1. App shellSidebar navigation, SvelteKit routes per page, and the focus month plus list filters kept in the URL so back and forward work.
  2. TransactionsUnified list with filters and search, the inline add row, the Month check rail, the calendar as a view toggle, and drill-in from anywhere. This is the biggest win for logging.
  3. Cash flowMonth and Year views with selectable bars, the category table shaded against typical, and cell drill-through. Activity is removed after this step.
  4. AccountsNet worth chart with a range picker, grouped accounts with status, the Log balances sheet and the net worth bridge. Net Worth is removed after this step.
  5. DashboardSpending pace, Needs attention, the bridge and recent transactions. Home is removed after this step.
Decisions for you

Open questions before building

Keep drag-to-arrange boards?

Monarch lets you reorder dashboard widgets, while its work pages have fixed layouts built for the task.

Suggested: keep arranging on Dashboard only.

Typical month or budgets?

Monarch is built around budgets. Your spreadsheets compared months with each other instead.

Suggested: use the trailing twelve-month median as "typical" now, and add budgets later if you miss them.

Where does the Sankey live?

It explains a period rather than a trend.

Suggested: a toggle on Cash flow's "Where it went" card for the selected period.

Phone layout

The sidebar collapses on narrow screens.

Suggested: a bottom tab bar with Dashboard, Transactions, Cash flow and Accounts, plus a floating add button.
