4 · What the mapping exposes

Decisions I could not make for you, and problems the mapping surfaced.

  • Fidelity holds 6 of 7 investment accounts. HSA, 401k, Roth IRA, Trad IRA, Brokerage and Stocks all take one hue, so the institution mark stops discriminating in the net-worth pane — it becomes a “Fidelity” block with one Schwab outlier. Suggestion: colour investment rows by tax role (--role-taxable / --role-taxadv) and keep the institution mark for cash and cards only, where it actually carries information.
  • Five institutions are blue. Amex #016FD0, Chase #005EB8, Venmo #008CFF, Schwab #009DDB and US Bank navy occupy roughly one hue. Strict brand is unusable for this set; brand-muted survives only because it pulls Venmo toward cyan, Schwab toward teal and US Bank toward slate. Flip between the palettes above and watch the cards pane.
  • Bilt is issued by Wells Fargo. Colour by the brand you interact with (Bilt) or by the issuer (WF)? I mapped it to Bilt — you log in to Bilt, not to Wells Fargo — but the mapping row lets you switch it.
  • Bilt’s own brand colour is near-black (#141414), which is invisible as a dot on the dark theme. It has to be re-lightened to a pewter, so it is the one institution where the mark is not brand-recognisable at all. Same story for the Fidelity black wordmark — I used the green from the pyramid mark instead.
  • Three colours are unverified. US Bank, Fidelity green and Bilt are not in the brand-colour databases I could reach; the values shown are the commonly-cited ones and are tagged unverified in the table. Everything else came from a published brand palette.
  • No logos. Fifteen bitmaps that need a light and a dark variant, are trademarked, and are illegible at the 10px the row actually gives them. The Monogram mark option above is the middle ground: brand colour plus initials, which is most of the recognition at none of the cost.
  • Renaming is not a leaf-only edit. Every Equity:Adjustments:* account mirrors an asset account name, and closed accounts still carry history — both have to move in lockstep, or the ledger stops balancing. The plan below covers both.
  • “BofA” cannot be de-CamelCased correctly. The app’s rule renders it “Bof A”. It needs an alias override no matter which naming scheme wins — as does Amazon401k (“Amazon401k”).
